Android pilots wanted

Help launch Orvix Aero on Google Play.

Before a new app can go on the Play Store, Google needs 12 people to test it for 14 days. If you fly and carry an Android phone, you can help it get there, and use the whole app free while you test.

Free while you testAbout 2 minutes to joinYour feedback shapes the app

How it works

  1. Leave the Gmail on your Android phoneIt has to be the Google account you use for the Play Store.
  2. Get your inviteWe email you a link to join the test on Google Play, usually within a day.
  3. Install it from the Play StoreTap the link, choose “Become a tester”, then install Orvix Aero like any other app.
  4. Keep it for 14 daysLeave it installed and open it now and then: log a flight, check the weather, upload your schedule. Tell us what breaks or what’s missing.

What you’re testing

A logbook that fills itself in, FAR 117 checks, your schedule from a screenshot, the commute board with gate alerts, weather and D-ATIS, training and currency. See everything it does.
