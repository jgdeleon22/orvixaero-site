How it works

Fly. Land. Tap confirm.

  1. It notices you flew

    Your iPhone’s GPS picks up the takeoff roll, the climb and the landing on its own. Leave it in your pocket and just fly.

  2. It fills in the details

    Departure and arrival airports, takeoff and landing times, total time, landings, night and cross-country time, and the tail number you usually fly.

  3. You confirm it

    The entry waits for you as a draft. Check it, fix anything, and save. Nothing goes in your logbook until you say so.

FAQ

Questions pilots ask.

How does it know I flew?

With your permission, Orvix Aero uses your iPhone’s location in the background. It looks for a takeoff roll followed by a real climb, then a landing. Driving, even on steep roads, doesn’t look like that, so it isn’t logged.

Will it drain my battery?

Location updates are batched so the phone isn’t woken constantly, and you can turn detection off anytime. You can still log flights by hand.

Does it need cell service or ADS-B?

No. Detection uses your phone’s GPS, and airports are matched on the phone itself. Drafts upload when you’re back online. There’s no ADS-B hardware to buy.

Which aircraft does it work with?

Any aircraft you carry your phone in. For U.S. N-numbers, Orvix Aero looks up the type and model in the FAA aircraft registry. Only the aircraft facts are shown, never owner details.

Can I bring my existing logbook?

Yes. Import a ForeFlight export or a spreadsheet, preview it, and Orvix Aero skips duplicates. You can export everything back out to CSV at any time, for free.

Who can see my flights?

Only you. Your logbook is private to your account and we never sell your data. Read the privacy policy for the details.
