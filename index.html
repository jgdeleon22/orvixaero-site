Questions pilots ask.

How does it know I flew?

With your permission, Orvix Aero uses your iPhone’s location in the background. It looks for a takeoff roll followed by a real climb, then a landing. Driving doesn’t look like that, so it isn’t logged. Nothing is saved until you confirm it, and you can always log flights by hand.

Can I bring my existing logbook?

Yes. Import a ForeFlight or Logbook Pro export, an airline crew log, or a spreadsheet. Still on paper? Photograph each page and Orvix Aero reads the rows for you to check. You see a preview first, and duplicates are skipped.

Can it read my schedule?

Yes. Upload a screenshot of your month or a trip, or paste the text, and check what it read before you save it. The screenshot isn’t kept, and pay, credit and sick figures are never read from it.

Who can see my flights?

Only you. Your logbook is private to your account, searches near you send an approximate area, and aircraft lookups never show who owns the aircraft. Read the privacy policy for the details.

You keep everything until the end of the period you paid for. After that your logbook stays saved, you can still export it, and it’s all there again if you come back.
