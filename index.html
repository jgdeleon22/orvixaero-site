Questions pilots ask.

How does it know I flew?

With your permission, Orvix Aero uses your iPhone’s location in the background. It looks for a takeoff roll followed by a real climb, then a landing. Driving doesn’t look like that, so it isn’t logged. Nothing is saved until you confirm it.

Will it drain my battery?

Location updates are batched so the phone isn’t woken constantly, and you can turn detection off anytime. You can always log flights by hand.

Can I bring my existing logbook?

Yes. Import a ForeFlight or Logbook Pro export, an airline crew log, or a spreadsheet. Still on paper? Photograph each page and Orvix Aero reads the rows for you to check. Or snap your airline’s time summary and your hours load as starting totals. You see a preview first, and duplicates are skipped.

How do flight alerts work?

Tap the bell on any flight on the commute board. You’ll get a notification when it’s delayed or cancelled, when a gate is posted, and when the gate changes, at both ends.

Can it read my schedule?

Yes. Upload a screenshot of your month or a trip, or paste the text, and check what it read before you save it. The screenshot isn’t kept, and pay, credit and sick figures are never read from it.

How does the pay estimate work?

Enter your hourly rate, per diem and monthly guarantee. Orvix Aero multiplies your block hours by your rate, raises it to the guarantee if needed, and adds per diem for your time away from base. Your rates stay on your phone. It’s a rough guide, not your paycheck: contract rules like credit and rigs aren’t included.

Does it show seat loads?

No. Loads live in each airline’s own systems, and sharing them can get employees in trouble, so Orvix Aero doesn’t collect or show them. You get schedules, live status, delays and alerts instead.

Where does the radar come from?

In the U.S., National Weather Service NEXRAD radar. Elsewhere, radar mosaics shared by national weather services. It’s for awareness, not flight planning.

Who can see my flights?

Only you. Read the privacy policy for the details.
