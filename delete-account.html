Delete your Orvix Aero account

Orvix Aero: Pilot Logbook · iPhone, Android, web and Windows

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Orvix Aero and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted right away. It works the same in the iPhone and Android apps, at orvixaero.com/app and in the Windows app.

Can't sign in?

Email info@orvixaero.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your logbook: every flight, route, aircraft, endorsement and starting total
  • Your schedule, trips, duty periods, saved flights and alerts
  • Training records and their photos, layover notes, your profile photo and settings

Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from what payment providers must keep. Apple, Google and Stripe hold their own purchase records as the law requires. Copies in our database backups disappear as those backups expire.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: on iPhone in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, on Android in the Google Play app → Payments & subscriptions, or for a web subscription in Orvix Aero → Settings → Subscription.

Delete some data, keep your account

You can delete any flight, trip, training record or photo in the app at any time, or delete all imported flights from Settings. To ask us to delete specific data, email info@orvixaero.com.
